# ANTIQUA — Investor Proof Scorecard

Статус: внутренняя система доказательств до fundraising.

## 1. Принцип

До полноценного инвестиционного раунда ANTIQUA должна доказать не количество функций, а четыре вещи:

1. люди действительно возвращаются к искусству и объектам без немедленного purchase intent;
2. участники рынка готовы публиковать inventory/creators через ANTIQUA;
3. digital interest превращается в measurable commercial intent;
4. event-driven acquisition не исчезает после окончания ярмарки.

## 2. North-star proof

Не GMV сам по себе.

Основной proof:

> доля новых пользователей, пришедших через предмет/автора/событие и совершивших второе содержательное действие в течение 30 дней.

Содержательные действия:
- deep Passport view;
- save;
- creator follow;
- gallery/dealer follow;
- collection add;
- inquiry;
- viewing request;
- offer;
- purchase.

## 5. Investor milestones

### Milestone A — Product Proof
Required:
- durable PostgreSQL live;
- exact-head deployment proof;
- no MEMORY_FALLBACK;
- critical browser golden paths green;
- event tracking stable.

### Milestone B — Supply Proof
Required:
- 2 partner LOIs or equivalent written pilot commitments;
- 10+ active professional participants in one real pilot;
- 300+ real objects or 100+ creator-led works;
- data onboarding completed without manual chaos.

### Milestone C — Demand Proof
Required:
- real external audience;
- repeat engagement;
- measurable saves/follows;
- D7/D30 retention visible;
- no reliance on team/demo accounts.

### Milestone D — Commercial Proof
Required:
- real inquiries/viewings/offers;
- at least some partner-confirmed transactions or attributable commercial outcomes;
- partner willingness to pay / continue / co-fund;
- clear pricing hypothesis from evidence.

Only after these four milestones should ANTIQUA frame a round around scale.

## 6. Claims we must not make before evidence

- TAM derived from entire Russian art market as if all revenue were addressable by ANTIQUA;
- guaranteed conversion uplift;
- guaranteed artist price appreciation;
- marketplace network effects before repeated cross-side behavior is measured;
- GMV including unconfirmed offline transactions;
- official partnerships without signed evidence;
- retention measured on internal/demo users;
- AI moat without proprietary behavior/data advantage.

## 7. Evidence room for investors

Minimum data room:
- live product URL;
- exact deployed SHA;
- CI proof;
- architecture summary;
- partner pilot briefs;
- LOIs / written commitments;
- anonymized pilot dataset;
- KPI dashboard;
- cohort retention;
- partner feedback;
- unit-economics assumptions separated from actuals;
- roadmap;
- cap table / legal docs when available.

## 8. Fundraising narrative after pilot

ANTIQUA is not “an antique marketplace with an art tab”.

Investor narrative:
- persistent identity of cultural objects;
- persistent identity and representation of creators;
- discovery graph built from collecting behavior;
- partner events as acquisition channels;
- transaction authority;
- post-purchase collection;
- primary + secondary market continuity.

The fundraising question then becomes:

> Can ANTIQUA become the system of record and discovery layer for a meaningful share of collecting behavior?

That is a much stronger and more testable proposition than “can we list more objects”.
