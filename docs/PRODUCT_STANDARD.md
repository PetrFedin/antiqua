# ANTIQUA Product Standard

## Benchmark
ANTIQUA targets the catalogue discipline, client confidence and visual restraint associated with leading international auction houses such as Sotheby's, Christie's and Phillips, while remaining an original product rather than a visual clone.

## Product advantage
The permanent object passport is the centre of the system. Auction lots, fixed-price listings, private sales, exhibitions and private collections are commercial or presentation states of the object; they are not the object itself.

## Published lot hierarchy
1. Lot number / department / sale
2. Maker, attribution, title, date and origin
3. Estimate and current commercial state
4. High-resolution media
5. Full cataloguing
6. Provenance with evidence status
7. Condition report linked to media
8. Literature and exhibitions
9. Specialist / review status
10. Shipping, taxes, buyer premium and transaction terms before binding action

## Trust rules
- Publication review is not authentication.
- Owner declarations, documentary support, remote expert review and physical examination remain separate statuses.
- Missing provenance is shown as missing; it is never generated.
- Reserve and proxy maximums are confidential.
- A material passport change after publication requires controlled amendment or re-review.
- Preview and demo records are labelled and never represented as live consignments.

## Experience rules
- Editorial, museum-like composition; no generic marketplace chrome.
- Images dominate but never replace cataloguing.
- No decorative 'verified' badges without a precise claim.
- Mobile users can see current bid / estimate and act without losing context.
- Private collection is private by default and independent of sale status.
- Every commercial object remains useful as a collection record after the transaction.
