# ANTIQUA Pricing Evidence Model v1

**Status:** STRUCTURAL — prices are hypotheses until accepted in real deals.

## 1. Objective

Pricing should emerge from observed willingness-to-pay and delivery economics, not from invented investor-deck numbers.

## 2. Evidence ladder

### Level 0 — Internal hypothesis
A number used only for planning.
Not evidence.

### Level 1 — Verbal acceptance
Counterparty says the range is reasonable.
Directional evidence only.

### Level 2 — Written commercial acceptance
Email / proposal / LOI confirms a price or range.
Still not revenue.

### Level 3 — Invoice issued
Confirms a buying process.
Not yet cash.

### Level 4 — Cash received
Strong pricing evidence for the exact scope.

### Level 5 — Renewal at same/higher economics
Evidence of repeat willingness-to-pay.

### Level 6 — Repeat across multiple independent partners
Evidence that pricing may be scalable.

## 3. Pricing objects

Track independently:
- Professional SaaS monthly fee;
- Gallery onboarding fee;
- Partner Edition fee;
- Event support fee;
- Institutional project fee;
- Institutional licence/API fee;
- transaction take rate;
- sponsor/activation fee.

Do not combine them into one blended “average price”.

## 4. Required record

For every quote:
- counterparty;
- segment;
- product/package;
- quoted amount;
- currency;
- scope;
- users/artworks/events covered;
- date;
- accepted / rejected / negotiated;
- accepted amount;
- discount;
- payment terms;
- invoice state;
- payment state;
- renewal state;
- evidence link/id.

## 5. Core pricing metrics

`quote acceptance = accepted quotes / qualified quotes`

`realized price ratio = accepted price / list or initial quoted price`

`discount rate = 1 - accepted price / initial quoted price`

`paid conversion = paid pilots / qualified commercial pilots`

`renewal rate = renewed paid partners / eligible paid partners`

`gross contribution = cash revenue - direct onboarding/support/provider cost`

## 6. Price confidence

HIGH:
- 3+ independent paid counterparties;
- similar scope;
- repeat or renewal evidence.

MEDIUM:
- at least one paid counterparty;
- written additional price acceptance.

LOW:
- verbal willingness only;
- internal assumption;
- free pilot.

## 7. Investor rule

Never show a “price point” without its evidence level.
