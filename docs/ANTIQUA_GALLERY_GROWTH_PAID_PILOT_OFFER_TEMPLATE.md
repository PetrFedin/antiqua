## 2. Pilot objective

The pilot is designed to test whether ANTIQUA can improve a gallery's digital artwork workflow across:

`Artwork / Artist / Research → qualified interest → inquiry → viewing → offer → outcome`

The pilot is **not** presented as proof of revenue uplift, ROI, market demand or product-market fit before evidence exists.

## 10. Measurement boundary

The parties acknowledge:

- engagement does not prove revenue causality;
- editorial exposure does not prove that a later buyer was caused by that exposure;
- passive audience identity is not disclosed;
- price acceptance is separate from payment;
- invoice is not cash;
- gross contribution is not accounting gross margin;
- a single pilot does not establish market-wide willingness to pay.
