# ANTIQUA v0.45 — Decision Intelligence

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED IN FEATURE BRANCH / VERIFYING

v0.45 deepens the existing Shared Intelligence Authority without adding a new top-level product pillar.

## 1. Collection Strategy

The collector sees a descriptive structure of their own saved/collected artworks:

- unique artworks;
- number of collections;
- concentration by artist;
- department / medium direction;
- period;
- origin / geography.

This is not:
- portfolio valuation;
- liquidity prediction;
- investment advice;
- automated buy/sell recommendation.

The purpose is to help a collector understand the shape of a collection and deliberately explore adjacent directions.

## 2. Gallery Portfolio Intelligence

The professional workspace now projects:

- artists represented in measured demand;
- artworks with signal;
- distribution across demand stages;
- highest observed stage by artist;
- active leads;
- response bottlenecks;
- top-artist concentration in the observed portfolio.

This is descriptive operational intelligence.

It must not become:
- a hidden lead score;
- a demand forecast;
- a ranking of human worth;
- passive-audience deanonymisation.

## 3. Evidence Coverage Map

Artwork Dossier now separates research coverage into independent dimensions:

- attribution;
- provenance;
- bibliography;
- exhibition history;
- dossier revisions;
- market comparables.

Each dimension can be:
- PRESENT;
- PARTIAL;
- MISSING.

There is intentionally **no single overall authenticity/confidence percentage**.

Why:
- different evidence dimensions are not commensurable;
- missing bibliography is not equivalent to weak provenance;
- market comparables do not prove attribution;
- a numeric composite would imply precision the sources do not support.

## 4. Decision value by participant

Collector:
- understand concentration;
- discover adjacent directions;
- avoid reducing collecting to shopping recommendations.

Gallery:
- understand which supply creates deeper demand;
- identify response/operational bottlenecks;
- compare artwork and artist demand depth.

Researcher / expert:
- identify exactly which evidence dimension is missing;
- prioritise source gathering and review work.

Investor:
- see one canonical artwork graph generating multiple decision products without duplicated truth models.

## 5. Evidence-to-economics hypothesis

v0.45 should eventually be tested against:

- Collector Intelligence -> D7/D30 retention;
- Collection Strategy -> collection expansion / research depth;
- Professional Intelligence -> response time / viewing / offer conversion;
- Portfolio Intelligence -> partner retention / renewal;
- Evidence Coverage -> research contribution / institutional reuse.

Until measured, these are hypotheses rather than proven causal effects.
