# ANTIQUA Paid Pilot Package v1

**Status:** COMMERCIAL VALIDATION PACKAGE  
**Purpose:** convert a real gallery / fair / estate / partner into a bounded paid pilot with measurable acceptance and commercial evidence.

## 1. Pilot principle

A pilot is not a free demo and not a promise of future scale.

It is a bounded commercial experiment with:
- named counterparty;
- signed scope;
- defined cohort;
- agreed price or fee;
- baseline;
- success metrics;
- acceptance owner;
- start/end dates;
- evidence pack;
- renewal / stop decision.

## 2. Primary pilot offers

### A. Gallery Growth Pilot
Audience: gallery / professional seller.

Includes:
- 20–60 artworks;
- 5–15 artist profiles;
- artwork dossiers;
- representation links;
- inquiry / viewing / offer workflow;
- partner analytics;
- D7/D30 evidence pack.

Commercial hypothesis:
`pilot fee = onboarding + professional workspace + evidence/reporting`

Do not hard-code price until accepted by a real counterparty.

### B. Fair / Event Digital Edition
Audience: fair, auction, association, cultural event.

Includes:
- bounded exhibitor cohort;
- artwork import;
- QR/deep links;
- event layer;
- save / follow / inquiry continuation;
- D7/D30 after-event report.

Commercial hypothesis:
`edition fee = event licence + onboarding/support`

### C. Estate / Artist Archive Pilot
Audience: estate, artist studio, foundation.

Includes:
- reviewed artist page;
- linked works;
- exhibition history;
- bibliography;
- provenance / research contribution workflow;
- representation status.

Commercial hypothesis:
`project fee = onboarding + reviewed corpus + professional workspace`

## 3. Mandatory pilot contract fields

- counterparty legal name;
- pilot owner;
- ANTIQUA owner;
- use case;
- cohort size;
- start/end date;
- fee and currency;
- invoice/payment state;
- scope;
- excluded scope;
- baseline window;
- KPI definitions;
- data/privacy rules;
- acceptance criteria;
- renewal decision date;
- evidence export format.

## 4. Success metrics

Consumer:
- artwork opens;
- artwork -> artist continuation;
- research depth;
- saves;
- follows;
- collection adds;
- D7 / D30 return.

Partner:
- qualified leads;
- response coverage;
- median response minutes;
- viewing rate;
- offer rate;
- won/lost outcomes;
- repeat engagement.

Commercial:
- fee accepted;
- payment received;
- renewal intent;
- support hours;
- onboarding hours;
- gross contribution after direct pilot cost.

## 5. Pilot evidence gate

A pilot is commercially proven only if all are available:
1. signed / accepted scope;
2. actual fee;
3. payment state;
4. measurable cohort;
5. start/end timestamps;
6. product telemetry;
7. operational cost log;
8. counterparty acceptance;
9. renewal / expansion decision.

## 6. No-overclaim rules

Do not call:
- a free proof of concept a paid pilot;
- a signed LOI revenue;
- pipeline ARR;
- a listing GMV;
- an offer completed GMV;
- engagement causality without evidence;
- one pilot repeatable PMF.

## 7. Investor evidence output

Every pilot should produce:
- pilot scorecard;
- baseline vs pilot;
- D7/D30/D90;
- pricing evidence;
- cost-to-serve;
- commercial outcome ledger;
- counterparty acceptance;
- renewal status;
- signed evidence digest.
